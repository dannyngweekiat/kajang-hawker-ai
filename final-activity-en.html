Final activity: Promote a product you sell

Create one ready-to-share WhatsApp or social media draft for a product you already sell.

  1. Choose one product you already sell. Write down its name, actual price and unit, one real selling point, and how customers can order.
  2. Fill in the prompt below and send it to ChatGPT to draft a short caption.
  3. Pair the caption with your own product photo or the clip you made in Lesson 9. Use the real photo if the video misrepresents the product or shows incorrect text.
  4. Show the draft to a partner. Can they tell what you sell, what it costs, and how to order? Fix anything unclear, then save the caption and photo or video together as a draft.

ChatGPT prompt

Write a promotional caption of up to three sentences for [product name] on [WhatsApp / Facebook / Instagram]. The price is [price and unit], one real selling point is [selling point], and customers can order by [order method]. Use only these details and end with an invitation to order.
