Introduce your stall

Describe your business, then add useful details.

  1. Open ChatGPT

    Sign in with your own account and find the outlined message box.

  2. Try asking this

    Copy this text, then paste it into ChatGPT’s message box.

    Fictional practice: I run A Mei Bakery in Kajang, selling kaya puffs. Suggest three ways to introduce my stall. Do not invent prices, ingredients or opening hours.

  3. Send your question

    Follow the marks: press and hold the message box to paste, then tap the send arrow.

  4. Add a condition

    Copy this text and send it in the same ChatGPT chat.

    More fictional details: RM10 a box, open Saturday 9 am to noon, pre-orders by direct message. Write a friendly introduction using only these details, without adding offers.

  5. Read and refine

    Check the location, price and hours. Follow up with “Please make it shorter.”

More things to try

  • Name your stall

    Suggest three memorable names for my food stall.

  • Welcome customers

    Write a friendly welcome message for my customers.

  • Say you have sold out

    Give me a friendly way to say we have sold out today.

  • Fill in the details

    Ask ChatGPT for three questions that would help introduce your product, then answer with confirmed details.

  • Shorten an introduction

    Shorten your stall introduction to two sentences, then ask for a friendlier version.

  • Find your signature selling points

    In ChatGPT, enter: “My signature product is [product], priced at [price]. Its confirmed features are [features], and my main customers are [customers]. Suggest three selling points and one short tagline. Do not invent details.” Check every claim and save a tagline for your stall.
