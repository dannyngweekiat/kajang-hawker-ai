Make a 10-second promo with Google Flow

Turn a product photo into a 10-second vertical promo with a product name, price and ordering message.

  1. Choose a product photo

    Prepare a clear product photo and three short lines: product name, actual price and how to order.

  2. Open Google Flow

    Open flow.google.com in your phone browser and sign in with your Google account. This lesson uses the website.

  3. Start a new project

    Tap the highlighted New project button.

  4. Open the video settings

    Tap the highlighted settings beside the prompt box.

  5. Choose Video

    Tap the highlighted Video option.

  6. Choose Frames

    Tap Frames to use a picture as the first frame.

  7. Choose a vertical video

    Tap the highlighted 9:16 option.

  8. Choose Omni 1.2 Flash

    Open the model menu and tap Omni 1.2 Flash.

  9. Choose 720p

    Tap 720p for clearer product details and text.

  10. Set the length to 10 seconds

    Drag the Length slider until it shows 10 Sec.

  11. Choose one output

    Tap x1 to make one video at a time.

  12. Add your first frame

    Tap outside the settings to close them, then tap Start. Leave End empty.

  13. Choose a picture from your phone

    Tap the circled upload arrow to open your phone’s photo picker.

  14. Select your picture

    Tap your product photo.

  15. Confirm the photo

    After choosing one picture, tap Add (1).

  16. First-upload notice

    If this notice appears, read it first. Tap I agree only after confirming you have the right to use the picture and agree to the notice.

  17. Add the uploaded picture

    Wait for the upload to finish, then tap the picture to add it as the first frame.

  18. Check your selected picture

    Check that the first-frame thumbnail shows the picture you chose.

  19. Add the promo wording

    Copy this text. Return to your existing Flow tab, press and hold the prompt box, then tap Paste.

    Create a 10-second vertical 9:16 promo from this product photo. Fictional practice details: kaya puffs, RM10 per box, orders by private message. Seconds 0–3: move gently closer and show “Kaya puffs”. Seconds 3–7: move slowly sideways and show “RM10 per box”. Seconds 7–10: hold the camera steady and show “Message to order”. Use large, simple, high-contrast text away from the product, with time to read each line. Preserve the product’s shape, colour, quantity and packaging. Add no people, objects, discounts or unsupported claims. No dialogue or music.

  20. Check the credit cost

    Open settings and check the cost for this generation. Confirm one output and that your balance covers it.

  21. Generate once and wait

    Tap outside settings to close them, then tap the circled generation arrow once. Wait for the clip to appear.

  22. Play and check

    Play the clip and check the product name, price and ordering message word by word. Make sure the text is easy to read and the product looks right before using it.

  23. Open the download menu

    Tap the top-right three dots, then Download media.

  24. Save the original video

    Tap Original size to save the video.

  25. Add your product caption

    Play the saved clip from Downloads or Files. Add opening details when sharing, and identify the visuals as AI-generated.

Start final activity

More things to try

  • Move closer

    Make a video that slowly moves closer to your product photo.

  • Move across the scene

    Make another version with a gentle sideways camera movement.

  • Feature another product

    Use a different product photo to make a promotion in the same style.

  • Give an order deadline

    Change the closing message to your actual pre-order deadline, keeping the product name and price accurate.

  • Show the packaging

    Choose a clear photo of the product and packaging. Request a slow zoom out while preserving their appearance and quantity.
