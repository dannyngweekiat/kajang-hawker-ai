Write menu and product introductions

Use the same confirmed facts for two versions and a spoken message.

  1. Prepare your product details

    Prepare the product, price, location, opening hours and ordering method.

  2. Try asking this

    Copy this text, then paste it into ChatGPT’s message box.

    Fictional practice: A Mei Bakery kaya puffs, RM10 a box, Kajang, Saturday 9 am to noon, pre-order by direct message. Write a menu introduction under 30 Chinese characters and a social introduction under 60. Use only these facts; add no health, certification or discount claims.

  3. Send your question

    Follow the marks: press and hold the message box to paste, then tap the send arrow.

  4. Revise both versions

    Copy this text and send it in the same ChatGPT chat.

    Make the menu version more concise and the social version warmer. Keep the price and ordering method unchanged.

  5. Check the details

    Check price, time and ordering details. Remove offers you did not provide.

  6. Prepare the video script

    Copy this text and send it in the same ChatGPT chat.

    Prepare a spoken line lasting about ten seconds using only: A box of kaya puffs is ten ringgit. Message us to pre-order. Thank you for your support! Do not add details.

  7. Keep your spoken line

    Copy this fictional practice line into your phone notes for Flow. Use actual facts for your own promotion.

    A box of kaya puffs is ten ringgit. Message us to pre-order. Thank you for your support!

More things to try

  • Introduce a new product

    Write a short introduction for today’s new product.

  • Try it in Malay

    Rewrite the same promotion in Malay.

  • Update your opening hours

    Write a message telling customers you are closing early today.

  • Try a headline

    Ask for three short headlines using confirmed product details, then choose the clearest.

  • Explain how to order

    Give your actual ordering method and ask for three simple steps. Check that no extra requirements were invented.

  • Create a trilingual product introduction

    In ChatGPT, enter: “Write short Chinese, Malay and English introductions using these confirmed product details: [name, ingredients, price, opening hours, ordering method]. Keep the price unchanged. Do not add certifications, health claims or other details. Keep the original dish name if unsure.” Ask someone familiar with each language to check the names and meaning, then save all three versions.
