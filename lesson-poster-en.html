Make a poster with ChatGPT

Create and save a product poster.

  1. Prepare your photo and wording

    Check shop name, product, price and ordering details. Choose one clear product photo.

  2. Compare two styles

    Compare these prepared styles. Choose one you like, then generate just one main picture.

  3. Attach your product photo

    Tap the circled plus to add one product photo.

  4. Choose from Photos

    Tap the highlighted Photos option and choose your product photo.

  5. Try asking this

    Copy this text, then paste it into ChatGPT’s message box.

    Make a square poster using my product photo. Preserve the product’s appearance. Include only “A Mei Bakery”, “Kaya puffs”, “RM10 per box” and “Message to order”. Do not add offers.

  6. Send your question

    Follow the marks: press and hold the message box to paste, then tap the send arrow.

  7. Check your poster

    Check price, wording and product appearance. Describe any correction you need.

  8. Open the generated image

    Tap the outlined picture.

  9. Tap Download to save

    Tap the circled download arrow.

  10. Draft a correction

    Copy this text and send it in the same ChatGPT chat.

    Do not generate another image. Write one clear correction request: keep the picture and enlarge the text so it is easier to read on a phone.

  11. Keep your avatar prompt

    Copy this prompt into your phone notes to use directly in the Flow app.

    @me Create a 10-second 9:16 video on a simple background. Say naturally and clearly: “A box of kaya puffs is ten ringgit. Message us to pre-order. Thank you for your support!” with one gentle wave. Keep my likeness and voice. Add no other people, dialogue or captions.

  12. Keep the video prompt for later

    Copy this video prompt into your notes too. Use it directly in Flow without returning to ChatGPT.

    Create a 10-second vertical 9:16 promo from this product photo. Fictional practice details: kaya puffs, RM10 per box, orders by private message. Seconds 0–3: move gently closer and show “Kaya puffs”. Seconds 3–7: move slowly sideways and show “RM10 per box”. Seconds 7–10: hold the camera steady and show “Message to order”. Use large, simple, high-contrast text away from the product, with time to read each line. Preserve the product’s shape, colour, quantity and packaging. Add no people, objects, discounts or unsupported claims. No dialogue or music.

More things to try

  • Launch a product

    Use the same product photo to make a new-product poster.

  • Take festive orders

    Make a festive pre-order poster with your actual order deadline.

  • Share your opening hours

    Make an opening-hours poster with your actual days and times.

  • Plan a takeaway poster

    Use confirmed product and packaging details to draft the wording and layout for a takeaway poster.

  • Plan a sold-out notice

    Draft the wording for a sold-out notice, using confirmed details if you include your next opening time.
