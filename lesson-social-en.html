Write posts and plan your week

Prepare two captions and a week of content ideas.

  1. Try asking this

    Copy this text, then paste it into ChatGPT’s message box.

    Fictional details: A Mei Bakery kaya puffs, RM10 per box, Kajang, Saturday 9am–noon, message to order. Write Xiaohongshu and TikTok captions of at most 40 words each, using only these facts.

  2. Send your question

    Follow the marks: press and hold the message box to paste, then tap the send arrow.

  3. Check and copy the captions

    Check price, time and ordering details, then tap Copy.

  4. Ask for seven post topics

    Send this in the same chat.

    Plan seven post topics, one per day: product, preparation, questions, packaging, opening details, product detail and feedback.

  5. Keep the plan

    Check the topics suit your business, then tap Copy.
