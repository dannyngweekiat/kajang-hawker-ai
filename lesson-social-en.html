Write posts and plan your week

Prepare two captions and a week of content ideas.

  1. Try asking this

    Copy this text, then paste it into ChatGPT’s message box.

    Write two Chinese social posts, each under 80 characters, using fictional details: A Mei Bakery kaya puffs, RM10 a box, Kajang, Saturday 9 am to noon, pre-order by direct message. Introduce the product in the first; give opening hours and ordering information in the second. Add no other offers or facts.

  2. Send your question

    Follow the marks: press and hold the message box to paste, then tap the send arrow.

  3. Give each post a focus

    Copy this text and send it in the same ChatGPT chat.

    Reduce repetition between the posts and keep one clear call to action in each.

  4. Check and copy the captions

    Check price, time and ordering details, then tap Copy.

  5. Ask for seven post topics

    Send this in the same chat.

    Plan seven post topics, one per day: product, preparation, questions, packaging, opening details, product detail and feedback.

  6. Keep the plan

    Check the topics suit your business, then tap Copy.

More things to try

  • Try another style

    Write two posts in different styles for next week’s new product.

  • Write a WhatsApp message

    Turn a promotion into a short WhatsApp message.

  • Plan three days of posts

    Plan three days of posts about one product, each with a different theme.

  • Answer a common question

    Use confirmed collection or ordering details to write a post answering a common customer question.

  • Announce a closure

    Write a temporary closure notice and check the closure date and next opening time.

  • Write WhatsApp business messages

    In ChatGPT, enter: “My product is [product], price [price], opening hours [hours], and ordering method [method]. Write four WhatsApp messages: today’s recommendation, pre-orders, sold out, and closure notice. Keep each within three sentences and mark missing details in brackets.” Fill in confirmed details and save all four templates. Check dates, prices and ordering details before sending.

  • Plan a simple promotion

    First decide the bundle, actual selling price, dates and quantity you can offer. In ChatGPT, enter: “Write a bundle promotion using these confirmed details: [products, price, dates, quantity, ordering method]. State the conditions clearly. Do not add discounts or gifts.” Check your costs and selling price, then save the promotion.
